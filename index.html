01 / The problem

Pet care shouldn't feel like a search problem.

Today, finding the right person for your pet often means scattered listings, word-of-mouth recommendations and very little visibility into who you are trusting.

01
⌕

No trusted first stop

Owners search “vet near me” in an emergency with no consistent way to verify who they are calling.

02
◈

No verification layer

Groomers, walkers and boarders are frequently hired through word-of-mouth, with limited standardization or accountability.

03
⌂

Services live in silos

Vet, grooming and boarding experiences are fragmented instead of living in one coherent pet-care journey.

The opportunity

Build the trusted layer first.

A single platform for vets, grooming, boarding, training and community — with verification strong enough for care where trust matters most.

03 / The differentiator

Verification isn't a feature. It's the product.

The central hypothesis is simple: a pet-care marketplace can compete without trying to outspend incumbents if it earns trust at the provider layer.

01Credential verification

Start with hard provider checks and a clear standard for who can appear on the platform.

02Background & quality signals

Build stronger confidence around non-medical providers through structured verification and feedback.

03Grievance redressal

Make accountability visible instead of leaving it to informal word-of-mouth.

04AI-native operations

Explore automated credential verification, fraud/review detection and dynamic trust scoring as the network scales.
